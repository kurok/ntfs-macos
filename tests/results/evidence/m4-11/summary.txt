mount_timeout: root cause found and fixed. With a slow probe the daemon gave a mount-approval dissent for macFUSE's own 8 MiB
backing disk (it appears while a volume is Mounting, with no content yet, and looked like a raw NTFS disk), which made macFUSE's
mount fail. The filter now recognises that disk (ntfsmac_da_is_macfuse_backing, U-DMN-DA-05) and the daemon never probes, claims
or dissents it. Before: mount_timeout-before-fix.txt; after: slow.txt. The journal_vm failure under host load is a different
case (that driver has no dissenter); it was not analysed further and is Open.
